## Description

To come up with solutions, we discussed as a group what was feasible given our technical capabilities and what actually addressed what we heard in our interviews. We talked through different ideas and narrowed them down based on how they solved the problems users described.Needfinding made it clear that students were not willing to move away from the tools they already use. This directly shaped our project, as instead of building a separate calendar app, we designed a system that sends event invites to users' existing calendars, thus meeting them where they already are. The decision to move to calendar invites also served to lower the barrier to entry and ease user onboarding, as users wouldn't need to adapt to a new calendar platform, and setup could be kept simple enough that more students would be willing to try it.
