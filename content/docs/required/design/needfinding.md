## Contributions

I participated in developing the Needfinding questions, conducting interviews, and developing the personas alongside my teammates.

## Description

To discover users' needs, we conducted interviews with Grinnell students, using a prepared set of questions we sat and wrote together about how they currently find out about campus events and what problems they run into doing so. We interviewed students from different groups on campus so that our findings reflected a broad and accurate picture of campus needs rather than just one type of student experience. For example, I mainly interviewed members of the Swimming & Diving team and International students on campus. The clearest takeaway was that users were not willing to migrate to a new calendar platform. Students were already using tools like Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, and asking them to switch to something new was not something they were open to.
